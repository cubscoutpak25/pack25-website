What We Do

Building character, citizenship, and personal fitness in the youth of Trinity, NC.

Pinewood Derby

Scouts design, carve, and weigh their own custom wooden cars to race on our official track. From needle-rail bodies to wedge designs, the competition is fierce and fun!

Outdoor Adventures

We host multiple family campouts every year. Scouts learn to pitch tents, build fires safely, hike local trails, and respect the great outdoors.

Community Service

A Scout is helpful. We actively participate in the Scouting for Food drive and local conservation projects to give back to our Trinity community.

Character Building

Through the Scout Oath and Law, we instill lifelong values of duty to God, country, and others. We prepare young boys to become responsible leaders.
