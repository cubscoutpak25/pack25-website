Discover the Adventure

Adventure Awaits in Trinity

Welcome to Pack 25! We are a dedicated group of scouts, parents, and volunteer leaders who believe in the power of the outdoors, community service, and hands-on learning.

From the high-speed thrills of the Pinewood Derby to sleeping under the stars at the Howl-o-ree and our Fall Family Campout, there is always an adventure waiting. Scouting is a family journey, and we encourage everyone to get involved.
