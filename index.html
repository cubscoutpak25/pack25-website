About Pack 25

Welcome to Pack 25! We are a highly active, family-oriented Cub Scout Pack serving the Archdale and Trinity communities. Guided by the Scout Oath and Law, we are dedicated to building character, fostering citizenship, and promoting personal fitness in our youth.

From learning essential wilderness survival skills to racing custom-built Pinewood Derby cars, our scouts are always on the move, learning by doing.

What We Do

Character Building

We instill values of duty to God, country, and others, preparing boys to be leaders.

Outdoor Adventures

We host family campouts, teach fire safety, and hike local North Carolina trails.

Community Service

A Scout is helpful. We participate in Scouting for Food and local conservation.

Pinewood Derby

Scouts design, carve, and weigh custom wooden cars to race on our official track.
