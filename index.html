Welcome to the Adventure

Pack 25 is dedicated to providing boys with an unparalleled scouting experience. From learning essential outdoor skills to building their own Pinewood Derby cars, our scouts are always on the move.

We are a highly active pack in Trinity, North Carolina, heavily supported by parent volunteers who ensure our boys have the guidance and resources they need to earn their ranks and grow into responsible young men.
