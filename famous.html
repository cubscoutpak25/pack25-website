Famous Scouts in History

Scouting builds leaders. Discover some of the incredible individuals who started their journey in a scout uniform and went on to change the world.

Neil Armstrong

Eagle Scout

Astronaut, aerospace engineer, and the first person to walk on the Moon.

Martin Luther King Jr.

Boy Scout

Legendary civil rights leader, minister, and Nobel Peace Prize laureate.

Steven Spielberg

Eagle Scout

One of the most commercially successful and influential film directors in history.

John F. Kennedy

Star Scout

The 35th President of the United States and the first to have been a Scout.

Hank Aaron

Boy Scout

Major League Baseball Hall of Famer and prominent civil rights advocate.

Bill Gates

Life Scout

Co-founder of Microsoft, software developer, and leading global philanthropist.
